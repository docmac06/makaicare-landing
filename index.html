How it works

One text. Makai makes the calls.

Makai is an AI assistant your family texts. There’s no app to download: it works right in iMessage or WhatsApp.

  1. 1

    Text what’s needed

    Mom, you or anyone in the family texts Makai in plain words, like “Can you refill my blood pressure pills?”

  2. 2

    Makai makes the calls

    It calls the pharmacy, the doctor’s office or the insurance company, waits on hold and sorts it out.

  3. 3

    The family hears back

    Makai texts the answer to Mom and to the family members she picks, so nobody has to ask “did anyone call?”
