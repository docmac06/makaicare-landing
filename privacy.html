Privacy

The short version: we use your mobile number to text you a signup link, once. We never sell it or give it to anyone for their own use.

Last updated November 3, 2026

What we collect when you sign up

  • Your mobile number.
  • Which sign-up box you used on the page.
  • The country your internet connection comes from, and the date and time.

That’s all. We don’t ask for your name, your health information or any payment details on this page. If you’re signing up a parent, we ask for your number, not theirs.

How we use it

We text you once with your link to sign up for MAKAI. If you reply STOP, we won’t text you again. Message and data rates may apply.

Who can see it

Only the MAKAI team uses it. Your number is stored with the two services that run this sign-up: Google (our private sign-up list) and our web host, Vercel (a private backup). They keep it for us and don’t use it for anything else. We never sell your number, give it to other companies for their own use, or use it for advertising.

How we measure this page

  • We use PostHog to see how people use this page. It records visits (where you scroll and what you tap) so we can make the page easier to use. Anything you type into the form is hidden from it, and it never receives your phone number.
  • Like every website, our web host keeps short-lived request logs, such as your browser type and country.

Deleting your number

Email manav@makaicare.com and we’ll delete your number from our sign-up form, list and backup.
